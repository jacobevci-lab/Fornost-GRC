# Control business impact

The native control assurance detail now explains potential business exposure using the same evaluated source snapshot. Its compact impact summary lists assets, BIA processes and explicitly classified critical processes. Expand either group to see owners, one deterministic shortest dependency explanation and exact record navigation. BIA recovery objectives are shown in hours; zero RPO is preserved and blank, invalid or negative values remain unknown.

The projection starts from the control's existing linked risks and its explicit process references. It resolves risk-to-asset, risk/control-to-BIA and the assets of directly linked BIA processes. It also includes BIA processes explicitly depending on these assets. It does not recursively traverse the additional assets of those newly discovered processes or invent connections to other risks. Multiple paths count a target once. A risk-origin explanation starts at the already-linked risk; the surrounding assurance detail supplies its audit/finding/automation context.

Only successfully resolved Connected GRC relationships are traversed. Ambiguous or missing references remain gaps, including those on reached assets/processes. No resolved dependency is not a declaration of zero business impact. Criticality is read from the record's explicit critical/Kritik classification; no severity, financial loss, likelihood or outage is inferred. The feature does not change a risk rating, approval, control score, stored relationship or access boundary. Existing source completeness warnings and evaluation timestamps still apply; this is not an immutable audit snapshot.

Functional coverage checks direct/shared dependencies, conservative traversal, ambiguous references, stable deduplication, zero/unknown recovery objectives and read-only scoring. Isolated browser QA uses real local records, exercises native asset/BIA navigation, excludes a newly ambiguous dependency, and checks TR/EN, light/dark and desktop/mobile. Real customer integrations and live deployment are outside this verification.
