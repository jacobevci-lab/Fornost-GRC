# Evidence-backed supplier contract extension — 29 October 2026

At main `898b5b60846acdd56fc2812854db5143c72537ea`, an independent reviewer cannot schedule a future review when a supplier contract has expired: approval correctly enforces the contract boundary, but the TPRM API/UI only sets that boundary at onboarding. A completed contractual extension therefore cannot be recorded without bypassing the workflow. This is source-confirmed; adoption and time saved are unmeasured.

Add **Record contract extension** to the existing supplier detail, available to the assigned independent Admin reviewer. The reviewer inspects current contract/review dates, chooses a later future contract end, records a reason, selects the executed amendment's canonical evidence version and confirms. This records an existing agreement; it does not execute or legally approve a contract. Preserve risk status, assessment, findings and review deadline. After recording, the reviewer can return to the existing assessment decision and schedule its next review within the extended term.

Commit the profile and linked core vendor contract end, retained event and evidence pin atomically. Require the inspected profile revision and previous contract date; guard profile, linked record, live account and evidence at commit. Reject non-reviewers, creator/business/risk-owner self-review, inactive/scoped users, missing evidence, unchanged/earlier/invalid/past dates and closed suppliers. Repeated requests return conflict. Source refresh retains drafts, requires explicit adoption and clears confirmation. Failed reads block submission. A lost response reloads canonical dates and history; a completed extension cannot be silently resubmitted.

Keep all extension history and its corresponding evidence accessible in supplier detail, with bounded pages and explicit read-error/retry states. Legacy profiles have empty history; never reconstruct it. No schema migration, new menu, palette change, contract shortening, automatic renewal, reassignment, risk acceptance, notification or legal signing.
