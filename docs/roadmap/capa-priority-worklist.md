# CAPA priority worklist

The GRC coordinator opens Findings & CAPA to identify an overdue action, its owner and the next step. The approved worklist gives the register the main surface, with source signals and global counts available below it.

Acceptance criteria:
- Rank the complete filtered register before pagination. Show expired risk acceptance and overdue active work first, work due today next, and keep closed items out of deadline warnings.
- Preserve exact record navigation. Expand one finding to see its next step, then open its existing action plan and independent decision workflow. Changing filters clears the expanded row.
- Distinguish a new, empty register from a search with no matches, a missing deep link, and an unavailable register.
- Exercise Turkish and English names, mobile access, keyboard expansion, CSV scope and role restrictions.
