# AI governance workspace

An AI owner registers the system and its supply chain, evaluates impact and privacy, records guardrails and submits a version to an independent Admin. Reviewers act from the decision desk. Monitoring a production system can lead to suspension, an incident or a corrective action. An unreviewed tool enters through Shadow AI discovery and requires a decision before admission or prohibition.

Acceptance covers the five bilingual, theme-aware views, stable AIS codes over existing model IDs, canonical vendor, asset, risk, control and versioned evidence links, independent review and durable decisions. A prohibited system cannot enter production. Limited and high risk require four tested controls and a report. High risk requires a documented impact assessment. Scope covers Fornost governance decisions and deployment records. External service shutdown requires an integration with that service.

The workflow draws on [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj), [ISO/IEC 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001), [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), [KVKK international transfers](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8143/Kisisel-Verilerin-Yurt-Disina-Aktarilmasi-Rehberi). The four intake risk classes support organizational screening. The gate records a human decision and supporting evidence.
