# Incident case workspace

An incident commander selects a case, reads business impact and the linked records, then opens a governed decision on that exact incident. The case, evidence versions, decision history and crisis brief share one incident ID. The queue exposes response and recovery breaches, search and the full lifecycle filter. A new register, a closed-only register, an empty filter, a missing deep link and an unavailable read need distinct outcomes.

Verify exact selection across two incidents, cross-module entry, retained evidence after subsequent versions, crisis roles/decision, independent closure, TR/EN and a narrow viewport. Use the existing decision and history endpoints, roles and source checks.
